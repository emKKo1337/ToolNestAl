## Where Lorem Ipsum Actually Comes From

![2,000 years of Lorem Ipsum history timeline — from Cicero's 45 BC essay through 1500s typesetting, 1960s Letraset sheets, to modern design tools](/blog/lorem-ipsum-generator-history.svg)

Lorem Ipsum text is scrambled Latin, derived from a real passage in Cicero's essay "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" ("On the Ends of Good and Evil"), written in 45 BC. The original passage is a genuine philosophical argument about the pursuit of pleasure and avoidance of pain:

> "Neque porro quisquam est, qui dolorem ipsum quia dolor sit amet, consectetur, adipisci velit..."
> ("Nor is there anyone who loves pain itself, because it is pain, but because occasionally circumstances occur in which toil and pain can procure him some great pleasure...")

Sometime in the 1500s, an unknown typesetter took this passage, scrambled the word order, and used it as filler text in a type specimen book — a sample document used to showcase typefaces. Crucially, the scrambling process cut words off mid-fragment: "Lorem" itself is not a real standalone Latin word — it's a fragment of "dolorem," sliced apart when the original passage was rearranged.

The text resurfaced in the 1960s when Letraset, a company that sold sheets of transferable lettering for graphic design, adopted it for its product samples, which popularized it widely among designers. When desktop publishing software (and later, web design tools) needed default placeholder text, Lorem Ipsum was the natural, well-established choice — a tradition that has now lasted over four and a half centuries in its scrambled form, and over two thousand years counting the original source text.

## Why It Still Works as Placeholder Text

The genius of Lorem Ipsum, whether intentional or accidental, is that it satisfies several practical requirements simultaneously:

**It's neutral and doesn't distract.** Because the scrambled Latin doesn't form coherent sentences in any modern language, a viewer looking at a mockup focuses on the visual design — typography, spacing, hierarchy, layout — rather than getting pulled into reading and evaluating the (fake) content. Real English filler text, by contrast, tends to distract reviewers into commenting on the words themselves.

**It has realistic word and letter length distribution.** Unlike naive filler like "aaa bbb ccc ddd," Lorem Ipsum's word lengths and letter frequencies roughly approximate real Latin-derived language, which makes it useful for testing how a layout handles varying text lengths, line wrapping, and typography at scale.

**It's universally recognized.** Any designer, developer, or client who has worked in the industry instantly recognizes Lorem Ipsum as placeholder text — there's no risk of it being mistaken for actual approved copy (mostly — see the mistakes section below).

## Lorem Ipsum vs Modern Alternatives

![Comparison of classic Lorem Ipsum, themed placeholder text like bacon ipsum, and realistic sample content — pros, cons, and best use case for each](/blog/lorem-ipsum-generator-usecases.svg)

Classic Lorem Ipsum isn't the only option, and different contexts call for different placeholder strategies:

**Classic Lorem Ipsum** remains the right default for professional client work, early-stage wireframes, and any context where you want the placeholder to be unmistakably recognized as "not real content" — minimizing any chance of confusion.

**Themed placeholder generators** (Bacon Ipsum, Cupcake Ipsum, Hipster Ipsum, and dozens of similar novelty variants) swap the Latin vocabulary for a themed word list while keeping the same random-paragraph structure. These work well for internal prototypes and casual demos where a bit of humor helps keep a team meeting light, but can read as unprofessional in client-facing or formal presentations.

**Realistic sample content** — actual (or realistic draft) product copy, real customer names, genuine data — is the most accurate option for final quality assurance before launch, since it's the only approach that reveals real content-length issues (a long product name that breaks a card layout, for example). It's slower to produce and sometimes simply doesn't exist yet at the design stage, which is exactly why Lorem Ipsum remains useful earlier in the process.

## Common Mistakes

**Mistake 1: Shipping placeholder text to production.** This is the single most memorable Lorem Ipsum mistake, and it has genuinely happened to real, well-known companies — Lorem Ipsum spotted live on a public-facing website is an embarrassing, widely-mocked launch error. Always add a specific content audit step before launch that searches the entire codebase for "lorem ipsum" as a final safety check.

**Mistake 2: Using Lorem Ipsum for final content review.** Because Lorem Ipsum has different word-length characteristics than your actual language and content, using it for final layout QA can miss real issues — an actual product name or headline might be far longer or shorter than the placeholder, breaking a layout that looked fine with generic filler.

**Mistake 3: Using themed placeholder text in client-facing work.** Bacon Ipsum and similar novelty generators are fun for internal use but can undermine a professional presentation — stick to classic Lorem Ipsum (or real sample copy) for client-facing deliverables.

**Mistake 4: Assuming Lorem Ipsum "means" anything.** Some people assume the scrambled Latin translates to something specific or hidden. It doesn't — it's genuinely scrambled and grammatically broken, not a coherent (if obscure) message. The one exception is that fragments of the original Cicero passage are still recognizable if you know the source.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Lorem Ipsum and where does it come from?

Lorem Ipsum is scrambled Latin text used as placeholder content in design mockups. It derives from a genuine passage in Cicero's 45 BC essay "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum." An unknown typesetter in the 1500s rearranged sections of this text for a type specimen book, and it has been the standard placeholder text in publishing and design ever since.

### Does "Lorem Ipsum" mean anything in Latin?

Not as written. "Lorem" is not a real standalone Latin word — it's a fragment of "dolorem" (meaning "pain" or "sorrow"), which was cut off mid-word when the original Cicero passage was scrambled centuries ago. The full scrambled text is grammatically broken and doesn't translate to a coherent sentence, even though many of the individual word fragments derive from real Latin vocabulary.

### Why do designers still use Lorem Ipsum instead of real text?

Because it's visually and linguistically neutral — it doesn't form coherent sentences in any modern language, so reviewers focus on the layout and typography rather than getting distracted evaluating the (fake) content. It also has realistic word and letter-length distribution, making it more useful than naive filler like "aaa bbb ccc" for testing how a design handles varying text lengths.

### Is it safe to use Lorem Ipsum in a real project?

Yes, as placeholder text during development — that's exactly its purpose. The risk is forgetting to remove it before launch. Always run a final content check across your codebase (searching for "lorem ipsum") before shipping to production, since this has been a real, embarrassing mistake for numerous companies over the years.

### What are good alternatives to Lorem Ipsum?

Themed placeholder generators (Bacon Ipsum, Cupcake Ipsum, and similar) offer a lighter, more memorable tone for internal demos, though they can feel unprofessional in client-facing work. For final quality assurance, using real or realistic sample content is more accurate, since it reveals actual content-length issues that generic filler text can mask.

This depends entirely on the layout you're testing — generate roughly the amount of text you expect the real content to occupy. If you're unsure, generate a slightly longer amount than expected; it's easier to trim excess placeholder text than to discover a layout breaks once real (longer) content is added later.

### Why does Lorem Ipsum look different across different generators?

Different Lorem Ipsum generators may start from slightly different base texts, include varying amounts of the original Cicero passage, or apply different randomization to word and sentence order.
